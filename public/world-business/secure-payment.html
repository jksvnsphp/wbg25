What is World Business Guide Secure Payment Service

World Business Guide Secure Payment aims to provide a safe payment service for all parties engaged in international trade. By partnering with an independent online payment platform (Click-Coin.EU), World Business Guide provides payment security to both buyers and suppliers.

Notice: On 01 April 2024, our Secure Payment Service is managing by Click-Coin.EU

How Secure Payment works

Start Order/ Wholesale Order
  1. 1. Buyer place Order on World Business Guide in Form of a "Request for Quotation" Serivice
  2. 2. Seller send Quote and get a Deal From Buyer
  3. 3. Buyer send payment +7% Secure Payment Service Fee to Click-Coin,EU
  4. 4. Seller Send Order to Buyer
  5. 5. Buyer receive Order and Confirms Click-Coin,EU
  6. 6. Buyer Payment released to Seller
  7. 7. Deal Complete
