Business to Business On WBG24.com

Business-to-Business – “B2B” – refers to commerce between two Businesses rather than to commerce between a Business and an individual Consumer. Transactions at the Wholesale Level are usually Business-to-Business.

The Dollar Value of Business-to-Business Transactions is significantly higher than Business-to-Consumer Activity because businesses are more likely to purchase higher priced goods and services and purchase more of them than Consumers Area.

A Bicycle Manufacturer, for example, will purchase a Truckload of Bicycle tires or a Coffee Manufacturer will buy a massive, industrial Beans Volume.
